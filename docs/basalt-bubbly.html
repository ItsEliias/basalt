Design review · volume seven

Bubbly Basalt

Ten deliberate departures from the contract — rounded, coloured, soft, loud. Same Today data as every previous volume, so the only thing that changes between panels is how far each one leaves the sleek, minimal language behind.

Each Cost line names which contract rules the panel breaks and which honesty laws survive. Bubbly is not the enemy of honest: Gentler Streak is soft and rounded and won an Apple Design Award for being kind and truthful. Big cards with fewer numbers is what sank MyFitnessPal's redesign — that is a density problem, not a corner-radius one.

If one of these becomes a seventh theme

Three could ship without breaking a single honesty law: Bento (your Tiles layout with colour on), Marshmallow, and Clay. They change radius, shadow, palette and typeface — all of which are already theme tokens — and keep receipt rows, real numbers, and the over-cap state in words.

Four need a rule rewritten to exist: Candy Rings (meter = ring), Pebble (mascot), Gummy and Blob (glow, gradients). Pop, Sticker and Soft are legal but each has a legibility cost you'd be paying daily.

The contract already carries most of what a bubbly theme needs. Two tokens are missing:
